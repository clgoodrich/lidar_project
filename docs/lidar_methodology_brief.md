# Lidar well-signature detection: methodology brief

Written 2026-11-26 for another LLM to read fast. Dense on purpose. Scope is the lidar pit, pad and road detection work behind the AGU poster. NISAR/SAR work is a separate subproject and is not covered (see `nisar/docs/nisar_pipeline_spec_9t.md`).
Companion file with raw metadata, result files verbatim and full source of every script in the chain: `docs/lidar_methodology_appendix.md` (machine generated by `notebooks/wellsight_v2/s7_analysis/_lidar_methodology_appendix.py`).
"WellSight" is the internal working name. Do not put it on anything audience-facing.

## 0. Read first

- Task: find the three surface signs an orphaned oil/gas well leaves in leaf-off airborne lidar: pit (collapsed cellar bowl with rim), pad (flat work area), road (access track). Detections are candidates, never confirmed wells. No site has been visited.
- Method: terrain rasters from lidar ground returns -> 7-channel stack -> small U-Net per sign -> threshold -> polygons -> matched to hand-drawn outlines.
- Truth: hand outlines drawn by the project owner on terrain renders in QGIS. The owner did not see the PA DEP well records while drawing. DEP records are not used to score anything.
- Headline, tile 9t, block-wise 5-fold CV, IoU 0.3, F2 cutoff: pits recall 0.928 (467/503), pads 0.912 (593/650), precision 0.63 and 0.59. Roads 0.982 recall on 735 held-out 40 m chunks.
- Transfer: pit models run on tile 613590 (no overlap with 9t) recover 0.911 of 153 drawn pits (sd 0.045 over five fold models). Precision is not computable there.
- Stale-doc warning. `docs/methodology.md` and `docs/development_history.md` describe the 2026-04..06 template-matching + XGBoost pipeline. It is superseded by the U-Nets. `docs/iterations/LEADERBOARD.md` has a banner and many STALE rows. Current pit/pad numbers are only the ann712 CV5 sections. `docs/03_las_inspection_report.md` is as-of 2026-04-13 and keeps old paths on purpose.
- Raw LAZ is not on this disk (offloaded to F:/E:). Derived rasters under `data/<area>/derived/` are regenerable from LAZ via `_build_derivatives.py` but only if LAZ is restored.

## 2. Terrain derivatives (`s1_build/_build_derivatives.py`)

Command pattern: `python notebooks/wellsight_v2/s1_build/_build_derivatives.py --suffix <tile>_05 --res 0.5 --crs EPSG:6346 --dem-method delaunay --bbox <xmin,ymin,xmax,ymax> --tiles "<glob>"`.
Pipeline (PDAL CLI via subprocess, Python bindings do not work here):
1. `readers.las` x N -> optional `filters.reprojection` -> `filters.crop` -> `writers.las` merge (9t merge 3.05 GB at 1 m run).
2. DEM: `filters.range Classification[2:2]` -> `filters.delaunay` -> `filters.faceraster` (resolution, pinned origin) -> `writers.raster`, float32, nodata -9999. Alternative `--dem-method gdal` = `writers.gdal` IDW, used for very dense mosaics.
3. DSM: first returns only (`ReturnNumber[1:1]`), `writers.gdal output_type=max`. CHM = DSM - DEM.
4. WhiteboxTools: hillshade (azimuth 315, altitude 45), slope (degrees).
5. NumPy/SciPy channels on the DEM (cell-based, so physical size depends on resolution):
   - `roughness_5` = stdev of DEM in a 5 x 5 **cell** window (2.5 m at 0.5 m).
   - `lrm_{3,5,11,25}` = DEM minus box mean (`uniform_filter`, size in **cells**). At 0.5 m, `lrm_25` is a 12.5 m window and `lrm_5` a 2.5 m window. Verified by correlation 1.0000 against the stored band.
   - `tpi_{05,15,25}` = DEM minus mean in a disk of radius 5/15/25 **m** (radius/res cells).
   - `openness_{pos,neg}` = Yokoyama 1998, 8 directions, L = int(25/res) cells (50 cells = 25 m at 0.5 m).
   - also `local_relief_10`, `tpi_grad_{mag,dir}`, ground density (uint16 count per cell), mean ground intensity.
6. Extra linear-feature channels (`_build_extra_channels.py`: slope residual, Frangi/Sato ridge, curvature doublet, structure-tensor anisotropy) were an experiment for roads. Not in the 7-channel stack.
7. RRIM (`_make_rrim.py`): Chiba et al. 2008. Red slope tint multiplied over a differential-openness (pos - neg) base, with Auld-Thomas 2022 colour stops (teal concave, grey flat, yellow convex, white to red by slope). 3-band uint8 `rrim_openness_<tile>_05.tif`. Display only, not a model input.

Grids: 0.5 m, 9000 x 9000 px per tile, origin at tile xmin / ymax. 1 m versions exist (4500 x 4500) for roads.

Model input stack (`features_pit_9t_05.tif`, `features_613590_05.tif`, float32, nodata NaN, band order frozen across tasks):
`lrm_25, lrm_5, slope, tpi_05, openness_pos, openness_neg, roughness_11`.
`roughness_11` is a mislabel. It is `roughness_5` (5 x 5 cells) at 0.5 m. Name kept so checkpoints stay compatible. Roads at 1 m use `roughness_5` honestly named.
Normalisation: per-channel mean/sd from the original training blocks, `data/9t/derived/05/feature_stats.json` (9t: lrm_25 sd 0.160, lrm_5 sd 0.0323, slope mean 8.84 sd 6.83, tpi_05 sd 0.120, openness ~87.1/87.3 deg sd ~2.4, roughness sd 0.193). Reused for all folds (see limits). mu and sd are also stored in every checkpoint and **inference must read them from the checkpoint**, never recompute on the new tile.

## 3. Labels, blocks, folds

- Pit label raster `labels_pit_9t_05.tif` uint8: 0 background, 1 pit floor (`pit_inside`), 2 pit wall (`pit_wall`), 255 nodata. Walls burned first, floors over them, `all_touched=False`. Script `s2_labels/_build_pit_dataset_v2.py` (refuses to reassign the split without `--force`; `--force` moves every fold and invalidates every checkpoint).
- Pad label raster `labels_pad_9t_05.tif`: 0/1 from `plat`. Built by `_build_pad_road_dataset.py`.
- Road labels 1 m: 0 background, 1 road, 2 drainage (`_prep_road_1m.py`; script deliberately points at a missing dir because the original 1 m stack was lost 2026-08-12 and the rebuild is not byte-identical).
- Blocks: 12 x 12 grid of 375 m blocks over 9t (144 blocks). 115 contain pits, 129 contain pads.
- Folds: block-wise, never by well, balanced on feature count with greedy deficit assignment, `CV_SEED = 20260727`. Pit folds (blocks/pits): 25/103, 26/100, 20/99, 23/100, 21/101 = 503. Pad folds (blocks/pads): 24/130, 27/129, 26/129, 27/132, 25/130 = 650. Pits and pads use different fold maps.
- Inner split per fold: 20% of the remaining blocks = inner validation, rest train. The held-out fold never touches epoch choice or threshold choice. Inner-val RNG seeded `CV_SEED + 1000*k`.

## 4. Models

Architecture (`_dl.UNet`): custom 4-level U-Net, base width 32, channels 32-64-128-256-512 bottleneck, each level conv3x3-BN-ReLU x2, max-pool down, ConvTranspose2d(2,2) up, skip concatenation, 1x1 head. 7,770,165 params (3 classes) or 7,770,132 (2 classes). 7 input channels.
Loss `FocalCE`, gamma 2, per-class alpha. Optimiser AdamW lr 1e-3 weight decay 1e-4, CosineAnnealingLR over the epoch count, AMP on CUDA, batch 16, 40 epochs. Best epoch picked by inner-val score. Augmentation random D4 (flips and 90 deg rotations). Sampling `CenteredPatchSampler`: patches centred on a labelled feature with random jitter, plus random background patches inside the split's blocks.

| task | classes | patch | jitter | focal alpha | notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| pit | 0 bg, 1 floor, 2 wall | 256 px = 128 m | 30 m | 0.05, 0.475, 0.475 | CV5 ckpts `data/9t/models/pit/unet_cv5/fold{0-4}/best.pt`, epochs 30, 22, 24, 31, 32. 57.3 min for 5 folds |
| pad | 0 bg, 1 pad | 384 px = 192 m | 40 m | 0.15, 0.85 | `data/9t/models/pad/unet_cv5/fold{0-4}/best.pt`, epochs 25, 25, 26, 33, 34. 159.9 min for 5 folds |
| road | 0 bg, 1 road, 2 drainage | 256 px at 1 m | roads chunked ~40 m, every chunk a patch centre | 0.10, 0.72, 0.25 | `data/9t/models/road/unet_1m_recall/best.pt`, wd 2e-4, best epoch 38, val road IoU 0.643 |

Inference: sliding window over the full tile (pit patch 256 overlap 64, pad patch 384 overlap 96), softmax probability, argmax or threshold on class-1 probability. Pit probability for scoring is the floor class. Polygonize thresholded mask (rasterio shapes), drop blobs under 4 m2.
Drainage is an explicit negative class for roads because channels resemble roads in relief. Fixing road recall by raising road focal alpha 0.60 -> 0.72 on clean 9t data worked. Multi-block training pulling in outside annotations hurt (overfit, under-confident).
Other checkpoints: `pit/unet_v2/best.pt` and `pad/unet/best.pt` are the older single-split models (train/val/test blocks, different mu/sd). `pit/unet_cv5_smrf` is the CV5 on SMRF-reclassified ground. `_retired/` holds ann527 checkpoints.

## 5. Evaluation protocol

- Match: greedy 1:1 by score, predicted polygon vs annotated polygon, IoU >= 0.3 (selection and headline) and >= 0.5 (strict). Pit match is predicted floor vs `pit_inside`. Pad match is predicted pad vs `plat`.
- Threshold selection on inner validation, twice, declared up front: F1 (recall = precision weight) and F2 (recall weighted 4x). The F2 numbers are the poster operating point. Probability grid 0.10 to 0.85 step 0.05. Selection IoU 0.3.
- Precision counts only predictions inside the held-out fold's block footprint (scored area buffered 40 m). Precision counts undrawn real features as false, so it is a lower bound.
- Containment (pits): predicted floor centroid inside annotated rim. "Locate" (pads): annotated pad contains a predicted centroid.
- Threshold-free: candidates = blobs at a recall-driven proposal cutoff (0.35 pits), ranked by score, leave-one-fold-out fitting, AP = area under held-out PR curve, 2000 block bootstraps (resample 375 m blocks within fold, same blocks for every model). FROC = recall vs false positives per km2. Calibration by ECE.
- Roads: line-level. `recall_clean` = recall on 40 m chunks whose entire parent road was held out (735 of 1,220 chunks, 221 of 344 parent roads, 43.07 km total) at threshold 0.20. About 39.8% of held-out chunks share a parent road with training chunks, so the clean subset is a workaround, not a fix. Road precision is not object-scored. Out-of-domain road scoring uses Wiedemann et al. 1998 completeness/correctness/quality, 5 m buffer, 40 m chunks.

## 7. History of approaches (what was tried, one line each)

- 2026-04..06: 1.5 km Venango tile, template matching (NCC) candidates + 88 features -> XGBoost/LightGBM ensemble with isotonic calibration. Cross-tile blind test on an adjacent empty tile. Superseded by semantic U-Nets.
- Instance models Mask R-CNN (ResNet50-FPN v2, 45.9 M params) overfit (best val loss epoch 1) on 74 pits / 51 pads. YOLO variants tried for pits and pads. Both abandoned for U-Net + connected components.
- 2026-07-02: instance metric bug found (precision 3-6% was a measurement bug: extent and class mismatch). Corrected 2026-07-27, real precision 0.54 to 0.69.
- 2026-07-27/28: pit and pad 5-fold block CV introduced. Single-split numbers were pessimistic.
- 2026-08-06: road labels fixed (+19 km faint road). Label recovery gained more than any architecture change in the road sweep.
- 2026-08-12: split and 1 m stack incident. `_build_pit_dataset.py` regenerated manifest, moved folds, 1 m channels lost, restored from E: mirror. Guards added (`--force`, SRC_1M trap).
- 2026-09-04 / 09-17: ann527 -> ann712 retrain. 2026-09-19: four-architecture 1 m comparison on identical folds. 2026-09-20: transfer test to 613590. 2026-09-23: ranked-list threshold-free PR analysis and operating-point policy sweep.
- Road sweep 2026-07 (clDice, boundary, alpha, orientation head, 0.5 m, linear-feature channels): two poles, connectivity vs precision, no runaway winner.

## 8. Limits and gotchas (state, do not hide)

1. Everything headline is one 4.5 km tile, one survey, one annotator style. CV measures stability. Only the 613590 test measures transfer, and only for pit recall.
2. Shared normalisation stats across folds leak 14 global numbers into each fold. Negligible, not zero.
3. Fold 4 (pit) and fold 2 (pad) inner-val draws were execution-order dependent in the first runs. Held-out sets identical. Numbers stand but are not bit-reproducible. Per-fold seeding fixed afterwards.
4. Precision is a lower bound everywhere. On 613590 it is invalid and must not be reported. The unmatched count is emitted as `n_unmatched_pred_NOT_FALSE_POSITIVES`.
5. Road recall 0.982 is at a loose threshold (0.20), on a clean subset, with no precision. Chunk leakage 39.8% on the full held-out set (measured cost ~1.5 recall points).
6. `roughness_11` is roughness_5. LRM, roughness windows are in cells, not metres.
7. Do not re-run `_build_pit_dataset_v2.py --force` or `_prep_road_1m.py` unless retraining everything downstream.
8. Large files (>=100 MB) are gitignored, never committed. Checkpoints `best.pt` and probability rasters are regenerable only by retraining.
9. Colour rule: no red/green pair with different meanings in any figure. Poster overlays use blue `#1A3CFF` (pit), yellow `#FFE000` (pad), white (road) on the red RRIM. The all-pairs validator has NOT been run on this blue/yellow/white palette.
