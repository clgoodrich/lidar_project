# Framework 3.7. Canada (scoped)

**Tier C.** Deferred.
**Evidence level.** (a) and (b).

## 1. Problem and objective
Alberta, British Columbia and Ontario hold large orphan and legacy well inventories. Coverage, access and record completeness are not established. The Ontario-first rationale is unverified.

**Objective of the first step.** A province comparison from actual metadata.

## 2. Gate before any build
An inventory table, `docs/iterations/canada_data_inventory.md`, with for each province the lidar coverage and point density, access terms and licence, well record fields (status, spud, coordinates and method), documented well types, and the local reviewers needed. A ranked recommendation that cites the table.

## 3. Minimum feasibility step
Search provincial portals (Alberta AER, Ontario Oil Gas and Salt Resources Library and Ontario GeoHub elevation data, British Columbia lidar and the BC Energy Regulator). Record what exists with links and dates. Do not download lidar. The USGS 3DEP catalogue is US-only, so no tile lists exist here.

## 4. What a full framework would add
Province-specific 3.0 transfer designs, local reviewers, licence handling, coordinate systems and datums per province.

## 5. Inputs, risks, resources
Inputs. Provincial data (unverified). Risks. Licensing, access, record completeness. Resources. 10 hours for the inventory. Needs local knowledge.

## 6. Documentation updates
Inventory doc, `literature/CITATIONS.md`, master plan.
