# Framework 4.3. Vegetation stress (scoped)

**Tier C.** Deferred.
**Evidence level.** (a). Without leak labels the result is a vegetation difference, not leakage.

## 1. Problem and objective
Leaking wells can stress nearby vegetation. Whether vegetation indices around pads differ from disturbed non-well ground is unknown. Mean leak rates are small.

**Objective of the first step.** Choose the question. Either morphology screening (does vegetation help find pads) or independently labelled leakage (does vegetation flag leaks).

## 2. Gate before any build
A written question and a label source. Check whether DEP complaint or inspection records with leak flags exist. If not, do the morphology question only.

## 3. Minimum feasibility step
- Multi-date optical availability for 9t (leaf-on and leaf-off seasons).
- Matched non-well disturbance controls (logging landings, old roads) with similar age and cover type.
- Index choice (NDVI, red-edge) and seasons.

## 4. What a full framework would add
An extraction script across seasons, a comparison with repeated observations modelled at site level (site-seasons are dependent), and an exclusion of canopy shadow and cloud.

## 5. Inputs, risks, resources
Inputs. Imagery, pad and control polygons. Risks. Disturbance confounding, cloud. Resources. 15 hours for a pilot.
