# Framework 6.1. CO2 storage and geothermal screening (scoped)

**Tier C.** Deferred.
**Evidence level.** Evidence layers for screening. Not a wellbore-completeness estimate.

## 1. Problem and objective
Carbon storage and geothermal projects must know about old wells that could be leakage paths. A surface-feature inventory could help screen a site. A surface inventory cannot establish penetrations, construction, depth or plugging records (40 CFR 146.84 Class VI requirements).

**Objective of the first step.** Define the partner decision and the subsurface information it requires.

## 2. Gate before any build
`docs/iterations/co2_screening_scope.md` naming a real or hypothetical partner, the decision (site screening for a permit application, for example), what the partner already has, and which part the inventory can add. If no partner decision exists, stop.

## 3. Minimum feasibility step
Design a layered package for a small demonstration area. Layers. Documented wells (records), candidate features with confidence and evidence level, and inventory limits stated first. A one-page summary with limits before results.

## 4. What a full framework would add
Partner interaction, area of review definition, comparison against subsurface records where available.

## 5. Inputs, risks, resources
Inputs. 5.6 outputs, records. Risks. Overclaiming completeness. Resources. 10 hours.
