**Why it matters.** It is another possible downstream use.

**Evidence level produced.** Surface evidence only. It cannot establish casing integrity or subsurface suitability.

**Inputs.** The 6.1 package.

**Architecture.**
1. A short note `docs/iterations/hydrogen_scope.md` that reuses the 6.1 layers and states what would differ.
2. No separate pipeline.

**Method outline.** Defer until 6.1 is established. It adds little distinct methodology.

**Acceptance test.** The note is committed.

**Risks and unknowns.** None beyond scope creep.

**Depends on.** 6.1.
