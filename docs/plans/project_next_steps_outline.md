# Where the project goes next

**Date:** 2026-11-11. Plan only. Nothing below is a result.
Source: `docs/plans/EXPANSION_MASTER_PLAN.md` and the per-idea plans in `expansions/`.

## 0. The open question under everything
- On 9t only 27 of 424 drawn pits (6.4%) have a catalogued well within 25 m.
- So "found a drawn pit" is not the same as "found a well".
- Four levels of evidence stay separate.
  - (a) Terrain feature matches a drawn outline.
  - (b) Historical record of a well.
  - (c) Steel in the ground.
  - (d) Confirmed well.
